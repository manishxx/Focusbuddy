Native notifications (static mock)

These are drawings of Chrome notifications, not real ones. Each has a title, a body and at most 2 buttons (SD-6).

macOS may hide the buttons, or the whole notification (SD-9). That's why every alert also updates the toolbar badge and the side panel, and the panel has the full set of choices.

With Private notifications on, the generic text is used and the task name isn't shown.
