Step 2 of 3

What Focus Buddy can see

Chrome showed these warnings when you added Focus Buddy.

“Read your browsing history”

Focus Buddy reads the title and site of your current tab, only while a session is running. Titles are checked, then forgotten.

“Display notifications”

Used for gentle check-ins.

  1. Open System Settings, then Notifications.
  2. Choose Google Chrome.
  3. Turn on Allow notifications.

Never: page content, typing, forms, or private windows (unless you turn that on).
