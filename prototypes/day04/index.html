Toolbar badge and tooltip

From UX guidelines §2.1. Badge colours are fixed (teal #0b6e69, amber #8a5a00, slate #4a5560, white text) because the service worker can't read the browser theme. The badge never carries meaning alone: the tooltip and the panel say the same thing in words.

You describe your task, how long, and which sites you'll use. While the session runs, Focus Buddy checks each tab's title and site on this computer. When a tab doesn't look related for a while, it checks in, as firmly as the style you chose. Titles are never saved. You can pause, mute or end the session at any time.
